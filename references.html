About the numbersSubsidence rates come from different studies, periods and methods (satellite radar, geodetic surveys and local estimates). Treat them as a range, not a single value. Some figures, such as the ~81 mm/year contribution of pumping, are model estimates.
Limits of this projectThis is a student awareness site, not an official hazard assessment. Quotes from news reports are paraphrased or translated and attributed to their source. Please check the originals before citing.
